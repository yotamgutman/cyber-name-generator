Before You Use These Names

Names generated by this tool are not guaranteed to be free of conflicts with existing trademarks, registered business names, domain names, or other intellectual property.

The exclusion list covers publicly documented threat actors and malware but is not exhaustive. A generated name may still be in use by an undocumented group, a commercial product, or a registered entity.

Before any commercial, public, or operational use, you are responsible for conducting your own clearance checks, including trademark searches (USPTO, EUIPO, or your jurisdiction's registry) and broader internet searches.

This tool does not provide legal advice. Use at your own risk.
